About the School

Olaoluwa Muslim Grammar School, Ado Ekiti, is a government‑approved secondary school founded in 1970 to provide quality education rooted in Islamic morals and modern academic disciplines. Located in the heart of Ado Ekiti, the school has produced scholars, professionals, and community leaders who excel with integrity. Our motto, “Knowledge is the beginning of fortune”, inspires students to seek learning as the foundation for success in this life and the hereafter.

History of Olaoluwa Muslim Grammar School

Olaoluwa Muslim Grammar School was established in 1970 by the Muslim community of Ado Ekiti under the leadership of Chief Imam and other notable Islamic scholars. The school began as a modest day school with a mission to combine Islamic religious instruction with a robust Western curriculum. Over the decades, it has grown into one of the most respected secondary schools in Ekiti State, known for discipline, academic success, and moral upbringing.

The school’s name “Olaoluwa” (meaning “The wealth/work of God” in Yoruba) reflects the community’s dedication to raising children in the fear of Allah and service to humanity. The first set of students sat for the West African School Certificate in 1975, achieving remarkable results. Today, the school boasts modern classrooms, a well‑stocked library, science laboratories, and an ICT centre. The alumni association (OMGSOSA) actively supports infrastructure and scholarships.

🏆 Inter‑House Sports Competitions

Annually, students compete in athletics, football, volleyball, and traditional games under four spirited houses, each named after Islamic virtues and founding patrons:

RED HOUSE
TAQWA (Piety)
BLUE HOUSE
ILM (Knowledge)
GREEN HOUSE
ADL (Justice)
YELLOW HOUSE
IHSAN (Excellence)
PURPLE HOUSE
SABR (Patience)

The annual sports fiesta (held every February) fosters unity, physical fitness, and healthy competition. The winning house receives the Olaoluwa Unity Shield.
